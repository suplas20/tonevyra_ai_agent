# T-026 Quality Benchmark and Regression Harness Contract

## Contract

- Dataset manifests are versioned and contain split, provenance, checksums, and license/consent.
- Train/calibration/test leakage fails validation.
- A-B comparisons use identical source and runtime/export conditions.
- Automated, human, technical, and operational metrics remain distinguishable.
- Human review is blind/randomized when used for comparative claims.
- Thresholds are versioned and cannot be silently relaxed.
- Strict evidence includes an executed benchmark run and machine-readable release decision.
- T-019 through T-025 regressions remain mandatory.
